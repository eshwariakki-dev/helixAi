# HELIX AI

### AI-Powered Connected Decision Intelligence Platform

HELIX AI is an enterprise decision intelligence platform that helps organizations respond to critical incidents using artificial intelligence. The platform analyzes operational disruptions, predicts business impact, simulates future scenarios, recommends mitigation strategies, and provides executives with actionable insights through an interactive dashboard.

---

## Project Overview

HELIX AI transforms incident management into an intelligent decision support system.

Instead of manually analyzing spreadsheets and reports, users upload incident data and supporting datasets. The AI engine processes the information, evaluates operational risk, predicts ripple effects across business units, estimates financial impact, performs chaos simulations, and generates executive recommendations.

The platform is designed for manufacturing, healthcare, logistics, supply chain, public infrastructure, and enterprise operations where rapid, data-driven decisions are essential.

---

## Core Objectives

- Centralize incident management
- Automate AI-driven impact analysis
- Predict cascading operational failures
- Estimate financial and operational losses
- Simulate alternative recovery scenarios
- Generate executive decision reports
- Visualize analytics through interactive dashboards
- Improve organizational resilience
